F-18 — weak password storage evidence (baseline commit 79f8a12)

Baseline app.py used a single unsalted MD5 operation:

  hashlib.md5(password.encode()).hexdigest()

Known test-password hashes stored by the application:

  alicepw  -> 15da1f78ad7d474862865bab1aab4d51
  admin123 -> 0192023a7bbd73250516f069df18b500

There is no random per-user salt and no work factor. Anyone who obtains the
users table through F-03 or F-08 can test password guesses offline at MD5 speed.
